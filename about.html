YINGYI KONG

Yingyi Kong has always possessed an innate curiosity to figure out how the world works, but her journey to the forefront of Computer Science was a deliberate and bold evolution. Rooted in a background of Translation and Interpreting, Yingyi spent her early academic years bridging human languages, developing a meticulous eye for structure and nuance. After enrolling at Northeastern University, this linguistic precision found a new home in the logic of code, where she quickly transitioned from analyzing text to architecting complex distributed systems. Her technical "debut" emerged through projects like FoodWise AI—a masterful blend of rigorous backend engineering and intuitive design that turns complex multi-agent AI into simple, daily utility. Today, Yingyi is defined not just by her full-stack capabilities, but by her unique philosophy: she is a builder who carries herself with the discipline of an engineer and the open mind of an explorer, creating solutions that are as stable as they are meaningful.
